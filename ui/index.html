      Security checklist

      Your balance is growing. These optional checks help keep a self-custody wallet safer — they do not block sending.

      • Recovery phrase verified Confirmed when you created this wallet, or after restore.
      • Auto-lock enabled Locks after inactivity so the passphrase is required again.
      • TLS certificate verify on Protects Electrum connections from man-in-the-middle attacks.
      • Know the wipe path Resetting local data requires typing a confirmation phrase. Funds are only recoverable with your written recovery phrase.

      What leaves this computer

      • Electrum — learns your transparent addresses and your IP.
      • MWEB peers — learn your IP (not your transparent address list).
      • Explorer (litview) — learns your IP when fetching spot price or fee hints; IP and a specific txid when you open transaction details or click “View on litview”. Wallet address lists are never uploaded.
      • On disk — transparent wallet metadata in sqlite is plaintext. Use FileVault (or other full-disk encryption) on this Mac.

      There is no Tor or proxy support yet. Turning off “Show LTC/USD” and fee-rate suggestions above limits litview contact to actions you start (detail view or “View on litview”). This wallet does not make you anonymous on the network.

      Connection

      Protects against man-in-the-middle attacks. Some community Electrum servers use self-signed certificates and only work with verification off. Turn this off if you run your own Electrum server for privacy — it stops the wallet from ever sending your addresses to public servers.
